## 2. Fixed during this audit

| # | Sev | Finding | Fix |

| A1 | **HIGH** | The app paid whatever amount the payout server/provider order asked for, though the payer had approved "about $X". | A3 | MEDIUM | Payout order creation shared the general 30/min write budget. On mainnet each order is a real Paycrest order. Live rate lookups weren't cached (the public estimate endpoint could flood the provider) | `ORDERS_PER_IP_PER_MINUTE` (default 6, tested); NGN rates cached 30 s |

## 4. What I checked and found sound

- **Contracts:**
  - escrow invariants and adversarial suite (no CRITICAL/HIGH);
  - relayer gas limits proven on cold state against real AUSD (mainnet + testnet);
  - USDC → exact AUSD on a mainnet fork.
- **Relayer:**
  - allow-listed methods only;
  - simulation before broadcast;
  - CONFIRMED only at finality with the expected event;
  - provider keys server-side and never logged (tested).
- **Payout:**
  - bank destinations signed by the owner and verified on the payer's device;
  - numbers AES-GCM at rest, never in logs (tested);
  - webhooks HMAC-verified and re-checked with the provider;
  - states only move forward;
  - "Delivered" only on the provider's confirmation.
- **Web:**
  - no raw-HTML sinks;
  - scanned and returned links restricted to the site's own paths;
  - nonce CSP with no violations;
  - claim secrets only in URL fragments.
- **Infra:**
  - shared-host rules respected (localhost-only ports, capped containers, no changes to other projects);
  - HTTPS, redirects and headers correct.
